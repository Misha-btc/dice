Why the bank can't die

Capped losses. No single win can take more than 0.5% of the bank, and no single block more than 20% — enforced by the contract, whatever players do.

Positive drift. Every bet carries a 1% edge and all of it lands in the bank. The bank can dip, but its long-run growth is a theorem, not a promise.

No locks, no admin. Shares mint and burn at the live bank price, anytime. Nobody can change the edge, pause withdrawals, or touch the bank.

Shares are plain fungible alkanes — hold, transfer, or sell them. Share price only moves with game results: deposits and withdrawals never change it. The edge is fixed at 1% forever — nobody can change it, in either direction. Design mock: simulated table flow, real contract math.
